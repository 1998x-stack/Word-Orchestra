# Word Orchestra

**Every word becomes music.**

Word Orchestra combines English vocabulary practice, keyboard input, and generative music. Each keystroke produces a note, each completed word becomes a musical motif, and phrases form musical sentences—while learning progress is measured independently from typing speed and musical output.

## Product boundary

Word Orchestra is the learning-led product. It prioritizes:
- Copy, active recall, listening, and contextual-use exercises.
- Separate language-mastery and typing-skill profiles.
- Spaced review and delayed-recall measurement.
- Deterministic, musically coherent feedback and session compositions.

It is **not** merely a free-typing piano or a rhythm/reaction game. Free-form keyboard-to-music exploration belongs to the separate [Piano Typing](https://github.com/1998x-stack/Piano-Typing) product.

## Design principles

1. Music motivates practice; it does not substitute for learning.
2. Copying is practice, not proof of mastery.
3. Measure delayed recall, meaning, spelling, and contextual use separately.
4. Adapt language and typing difficulty independently.
5. Avoid punitive streaks, loss aversion, and manipulative reward loops.
